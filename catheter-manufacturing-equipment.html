Catheter Manufacturing Equipment

Home / Products & Services / Catheter Manufacturing Equipment

Catheter manufacturing equipment from Thor Medical Automation — purpose-built machines that bring precision, repeatability and throughput to your catheter production line.

Based in Brooklyn Park, MN, Thor Medical Automation designs and builds automated equipment for medical device manufacturers, from start-ups to established OEMs. Their leadership team brings over 100 years of combined MedTech experience, and every machine is backed by dedicated support, including free troubleshooting for equipment under warranty.

Equipment Lineup

Reflow Vertical Laminators

Vertical reflow ovens for catheter shaft lamination, with precise temperature control and uniform heat distribution for consistent, high-quality results.

Mandrel Pullers

Smooth, consistent mandrel and liner removal that protects product integrity, reduces operator strain and extends tooling life.

Braid Annealers

Consistent heat treatment of braided reinforcement for improved flexibility and stability, with reliable results across high-volume runs.

Coil Winders

High-performance coilers with consistent tension control and precise winding across a range of wire sizes and materials.

Femto Laser Systems

Femtosecond laser cutting systems for clean edges, fine detail and repeatable results on hypotubes and other precision components.

Laser Welders

Clean, consistent laser welds with minimal distortion, even on delicate or complex medical device components.
