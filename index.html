About Us

Custom Engineered Solutions, Backed by Decades of Expertise

The Alexander Sales Group is a manufacturers representative agency providing custom engineered solutions for medical device OEMs and contract manufacturers alike. Minnesota's Medical Alley is a major hub for contract manufacturing, and we connect our component manufacturers to both sides of the industry — delivering outsourced solutions that work in concert with your project requirements.

Headquartered in St. Paul and led by Bill Alexander, a lifelong Twin Cities resident with decades of experience selling contract manufacturing and custom components in this territory.

  • Thermoplastic, braided, fluoroplastic, stainless & nitinol tubing
  • Machined processes and component finishing
  • Contract manufacturing for finished device assemblies
  • Electromechanical assembly services

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our medical device components and services.

What does Alexander Sales Group do?
Alexander Sales Group is a manufacturers' representative agency based in St. Paul, Minnesota that supplies custom-engineered components for medical device OEMs and contract manufacturers. We connect medtech companies with specialized manufacturers across tubing, wire, coils, mandrels, balloons, laser machining, and catheter processing.
What products and services do you offer?
Catheter liners & FEP heat shrink, medical tubing, catheter manufacturing equipment, laser machining, medical balloons, polyimide tubing, mandrels, micro coils & assemblies, copper and silver plated wire, and flat & custom shaped wire.
Where are you located and what areas do you serve?
We are headquartered in St. Paul, MN and serve the Twin Cities metro and Minnesota's Medical Alley — including Minneapolis, Maple Grove, Plymouth, Brooklyn Park, Mounds View, Arden Hills, and Roseville — plus Wisconsin and the broader Upper Midwest.
What is a manufacturers' representative?
A manufacturers' representative is an independent agency that represents multiple specialized manufacturers, giving you a single, local point of contact to source components from best-in-class suppliers without managing many separate vendor relationships.
Which manufacturers do you represent?
Keystone Medical Technologies (tubing & polyimide), Proto Lase (laser processing), Medical Component Specialists (mandrels), Thermetic Products (micro coils), Little Falls Alloys (specialty wire), United Wire Company (flat braid wire), and Thor Medical Automation (catheter manufacturing equipment).
What industries and applications do you support?
We support medical device OEMs and contract manufacturers building catheter-based, minimally invasive, and other precision medical devices.
How do I request a quote?
Call Bill Alexander at 612-501-0415, email bill@alexandersalesgroup.com, or use the contact form on this site. Sending drawings or specifications helps us respond quickly.
